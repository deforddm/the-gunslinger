# The Gunslinger — Scene List

Stage 0 · v0.1 · Mon Sep 28, 2026 · for David's review

Every scene in play order. Each lists the Spine beats it covers (see `canon.md` §5), its kind (**Road** = present tense; **Memory** = flashback panel the player enters and leaves), the flavor choices, the art and sound, any mechanic, and any Constant Reader sigil. Target playtime in brackets. Choices never change outcomes; **KHEF** marks choices that move the hidden ledger (+ toward the boy, − toward the Tower).

Palette tokens: `bone` `dust` `mustard` `sienna` `sunline` `peach` `lavender` `smoke` `umber` `acid` `sulphur` `moss` `blood`.

## Title screen

- The first sentence (quotation 1), set alone on `bone` over a slow `mustard` horizon; a speck moves on it and never arrives.
- *For adult readers. A faithful walkthrough of a violent book.*

**Tull set piece (1.12).** Hold to steady, release to fire; each release is one shot. The townsfolk keep coming whether the player fires or not; if the player stops, Roland does not. The music (if any) is the loudest in the game and cuts to nothing on 58. Then 1.13.

KHEF decides only one line: whether Roland looks down.
