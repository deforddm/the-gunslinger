# The Gunslinger

A playable walkthrough of *The Dark Tower: The Gunslinger* by Stephen King, following the original 1982 Donald M. Grant text. Five chapters, 48 scenes, about two hours. For adult readers.

**Play:** https://deforddm.github.io/the-gunslinger/

- Locked canon with flavor choices: what Roland does and notices changes; what happens does not.
- Water in the desert, the draw, the Tull street, the jawbone, the drop, the seven cards, the vision.
- An optional Constant Reader layer of echoes from further down the road.
- One self-contained page. Works offline once loaded. Saves at every scene; save codes carry your place between devices.

`docs/canon.md` is the 1982 canon bible the game is written against. `docs/scenes.md` is the scene list.
